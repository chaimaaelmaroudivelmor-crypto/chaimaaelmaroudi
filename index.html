01 — Who I am

Operations first, engineering next

I work in operations and service support, holding the daily, weekly and monthly rhythm of a technical service department together.

When a client opens a ticket, I follow it until a technician closes it. In between I coordinate with service leaders, keep contract and portfolio records current, classify new units, and handle client contact by email, telephone and in person.

The work runs across every part of the department, so I've learned how the whole service cycle fits together rather than one section of it.

The part I enjoy most is improving the process rather than only running it. I notice where things break, propose changes, and several of my proposals have been adopted and are now standard.

That instinct is what led me toward technology. I'm studying computer science alongside the job, aiming to build the operational tools I currently work around — I know that problem from the inside rather than from a case study.
