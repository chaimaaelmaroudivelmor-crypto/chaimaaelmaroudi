01, Who I am

Operations first, engineering next

Most of my week is spent with data. I pull ticket exports from the service platform and run Python scripts over them to see which interventions are closing on time, which are not, and where the delays are concentrated.

The rest of the role is everything that surrounds that: coordinating technicians and service leaders, keeping contract and portfolio records current, classifying new units, and speaking with clients by email, telephone and in person.

Because the work runs across the whole department, I have learned how the full service cycle fits together rather than one section of it.

The part I enjoy most is improving the process rather than only running it. I look for where things break, propose changes, and several of my proposals have been adopted and are now standard.

That is what pulled me toward technology. I am studying computer science alongside the job, building on the analysis work I already do, and aiming to build the operational tools I currently work around.

02, What I bring

Three things I do well

01

Turning exports into answers

I take raw ticket data out of the service platform and analyse it with Python, producing a clear picture of which interventions perform and which do not. Judgement based on numbers, not impressions.

03
